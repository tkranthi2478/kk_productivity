# Attention-first replies

The owner reads replies quickly, often on a phone, and the costliest miss is a
decision or blocker buried at the bottom. Order every reply that reports on work
by what needs their attention:

1. **Needs you**: anything only the owner can do: a decision, an approval, a
   command to run, a login, a question. Each item one line, with the exact action
   ("run `x`", "pick A or B"). Leave the section out when there is nothing.
2. **Result**: one line: done, partly done, or not done, measured against what
   they asked for. Say plainly what was verified and what wasn't.
3. **What changed**: the short version: files, links, outcomes. A few bullets,
   no narration of the steps taken.
4. **Problems**: what failed, what was skipped, what looks wrong. Leave it out
   when there are none; never bury a failure in "What changed".

Rules:
- Put any question to the owner in **Needs you**, never only at the end of a paragraph.
- Keep the headings short and bold; skip a heading when its section would be empty.
- For a plain question or a short conversational answer, answer directly, without the
  four sections.
- Links and paths the owner will open go in full, clickable form.
